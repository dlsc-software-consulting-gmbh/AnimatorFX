THE CREATIVE TOOLKIT ✳

Everything you need
to make a move.

From a quick interaction to a rich animated scene, AnimatorFX brings your workflow together in one thoughtfully designed editor.

⌁
01 / CREATE

A canvas for your imagination.

Draw rectangles, ellipses, lines, polygons, stars and Bézier paths. Add text, images and groups; style them with solid or gradient fills, strokes and dashes.

VECTOR TOOLSTEXT & IMAGESSNAPPING
◇
02 / ANIMATE

Make every frame count.

Use a layer-based timeline with keyframes, in/out bars, markers and scrubbing. Auto-key and per-property controls make animation feel natural.

⌇
03 / REFINE

Motion with feeling.

Fine-tune timing with a cubic Bézier easing editor. Bring scenes to life with motion paths, path morphing and trim paths.

◒
04 / COMPOSE

Details make the difference.

Combine clip and alpha masks with blur, drop shadow and glow. Group layers and shape a scene that is entirely your own.

♫
05 / SOUND

Give motion a soundtrack.

Add audio layers and sound cues, see waveforms, and animate volume and balance. Audio follows the animation clock and playback rate.

▷
06 / VIDEO

Bring video into the mix.

Import MP4 and M4V video layers with picture and sound together. Transform, mask, loop and animate them on the timeline.

▦
07 / WORKFLOW

Stay in your flow.

Zoom, pan, snap, copy and paste layers or keyframes, and undo and redo. Choose a light or dark theme, or follow your system.

↺
08 / RECOVERY

Pick up where you left off.

Session restore remembers your workspace, while automatic recovery copies help protect unsaved changes.

MADE TO PLAY WELL WITH OTHERS ✳

There’s a lot to
love about Lottie.

Bring existing Lottie JSON animations into AnimatorFX, or export your work as Lottie JSON or a dotLottie archive. SVG and FXML imports are supported too.

Lottie support is powered by Lottie4J, an open-source Java library for working with Lottie animations. Because formats differ, the editor reports anything skipped or approximated during import and export.

FROM EDITOR TO APPLICATION ✳

Made for creators.
Built for developers.

Design in the editor, save as an .afx file, and play it directly in your JavaFX application with the lightweight player.

01

Native JavaFX playback

AnimationView is a resizable, CSS-styleable control. Play, pause, seek, loop, reverse, adjust the rate and choose how your animation fits.

02

Your UI, animated

Named slots let an animation drive your own JavaFX nodes, embedded in the animation or in place in your existing layout.

03

Made to integrate

Play named marker ranges and control playback programmatically. Scene-graph rendering stays crisp as it scales. The runtime supports Java and JavaFX 21+.
